The ACL is a strong band inside your knee that keeps it steady when you walk, run, or turn. A tear means the band has stretched too far and split apart. This is a common injury, and most people heal well with the right care. Your care team will guide you to the best next step for you.

This guide is for learning only. For advice about your own knee, please talk with your care team.
